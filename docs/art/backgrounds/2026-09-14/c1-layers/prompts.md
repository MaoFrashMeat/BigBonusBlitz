# C-1 3層制作プロンプト

2026-09-14、ユーザーがv2の方向で進行を承認。組み込みimage_gen、各レイヤーを別々の呼び出しで作成。参照は `../forest-reference-v2.png`。
左右に透明な自然な空間を持つモジュール＋Unityの連続地面で構成する。異なる物体同士をブレンドして継ぎ目を隠さない。

## far

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Make ONE production 2D parallax game layer, PNG with genuine RGBA TRANSPARENCY, landscape 3:1. Input image is the approved STYLE reference only. Match its hard angular painted color planes, deep cool teal shadows, moss greens, economical foliage clusters and monumental ancient forest. No photoreal texture, no black outline, no paper grain. Neutral diffuse surface lighting; no sunbeams, haze veil, sky color, weather, glow, cast sunlight, people, words, UI or checkerboard painted into the art. This image will be composited over a separately rendered sky and ground in Unity. Empty areas MUST be actually transparent. Leave BOTH leftmost and rightmost 5% of canvas COMPLETELY transparent over full height: ALL silhouettes terminate naturally inside that margin, nothing touches either side edge. This makes an isolated scenery module repeatable without a cut. Keep canvas full width and height; do not crop to content. Layer: FAR DISTANT WOODLAND ONLY. A broad asymmetric group of several towering slender ancient tree trunks, partial broken arches far away, and upper canopy silhouettes. Trees span from near top to y=86%, light desaturated blue teal palette, broad simple flat shapes and subtle faceted strokes. Transparent openings between trunks and above lower small shrubs, pale trees themselves are opaque with actual transparent space around them. No foreground roots, no path, no ground strip, no near rocks, no bright chartreuse highlights. Foliage at top has naturally rounded and angular ends inside side margins. Bottom 12% fully transparent. Shape complexity much simpler than the middle distance.
```
