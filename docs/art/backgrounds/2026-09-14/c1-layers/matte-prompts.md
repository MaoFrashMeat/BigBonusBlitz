# 出力形式の修正

生成器へ実アルファを2回指定したが、どちらもRGBで返った（初回はチェック柄、修正は黒）。チェック柄版は不採用。採用するfar.pngは黒マットRGB。PNGそのものを透過済みと記載しない。
Unityの専用シェーダーで黒マットをアルファへ変換する。物体は有彩色で最大チャンネル45/255以上、空白は0。左右ガード部分のアルファを0にし、独立モジュールの輪郭と連続地面を組み合わせる。

## middle

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Make ONE production 2D parallax game layer, PNG on a PERFECTLY SOLID PURE BLACK #000000 MATTE, landscape 3:1. Input image is the approved STYLE reference only. Match its hard angular painted color planes, deep cool teal shadows, moss greens, economical foliage clusters and monumental ancient forest. No photoreal texture, no black outline, no paper grain. Neutral diffuse surface lighting; no sunbeams, haze veil, sky color, weather, glow, cast sunlight, people, words, UI or checkerboard painted into the art. This image will be composited over a separately rendered sky and ground in Unity. Empty areas MUST be pure black. Leave BOTH leftmost and rightmost 5% of canvas COMPLETELY pure black over full height: ALL silhouettes terminate naturally inside that margin, nothing touches either side edge. This makes an isolated scenery module repeatable without a cut. Keep canvas full width and height; do not crop to content. Layer: MIDDLE SCENERY ONLY. Two or three enormous forest trunks of different shapes, an old broken moss covered stone arch and separated ruined pillars, clusters of green foliage and small muted magenta accent leaves. Trunks and arch rise across the upper 78% of canvas, roots end near y=86%, bottom 12% empty pure black. Large genuinely PURE BLACK openings through the arch, between branches and between trees. Trees must not form an opaque rectangular wall. Some canopy crops the TOP only. Keep all left/right object contours naturally closed 5% inward. No distant trees visible through holes, no distant background, no path or ground across the full canvas. Detailed enough to match the approved concept but painted in broad angular planes with limited colors. Moderate neutral olive moss, cool teal-brown trunks, no yellow sunlight patches. IMPORTANT OUTPUT OVERRIDE: all empty space must be uniform pure black RGB(0,0,0), no transparency checkerboard. This is a chroma/luma key production source for a Unity shader. Forest objects must NEVER contain black: their darkest plane is dark teal with max RGB channel at least 45/255. Diffuse green moss highlights, no yellow light patches. Natural left/right empty black margins essential.
```
