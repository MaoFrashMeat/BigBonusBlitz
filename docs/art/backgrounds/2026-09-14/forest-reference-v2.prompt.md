# 参考画像に合わせた森の画風見本・プロンプト

- 方法: 組み込み `image_gen`。ユーザー提供3枚をスタイル参照として入力。
- 出力: `forest-reference-v2.png`。C-1「霧の森」を題材にした横長の画風見本。
- 参考3を主に形と森林構成へ、参考1を色対比へ、参考2を岩の面とエッジへ使用。
- 完成した透過レイヤー／シームレスタイルではない。光と霞を含む合成状態の目標画。

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: a NEW wide background art-direction sample for the 2D side-scrolling fantasy RPG BigBonusBlitz. One single panoramic illustration, aspect ratio 3:1, ideally 2304x768. No panels, no labels, no UI.
Input images: all THREE supplied images are STYLE REFERENCES ONLY, not edit targets. Reference 1 establishes jewel violet/cyan accent color contrast and bold simplified stone forms. Reference 2 establishes angular planar rock painting, economical sharp brush marks and atmospheric depth. Reference 3 is the PRIMARY reference for forest foliage shape language, giant vertical tree scale, ancient overgrown arches, broad painterly color masses, deep cool green shadows and luminous yellow-green canopy. Preserve their common bold hand-painted anime fantasy art language, NOT their exact composition or specific landmarks. Do not imitate the previous soft pastoral grassland comparison.
Primary request: design C-1, 'mist forest', an ancient forest traversed by a traveler, with weathered ruin fragments half swallowed by enormous trees. This is a reference-aligned TASTE SAMPLE, not a finished production tile. Beautiful Japanese animation concept background, adventurous, mysterious and luminous, not realistic, not a children's picture book.
Composition: translate the towering monumental scale of the portrait references to a HORIZONTAL SIDE-SCROLLING game background. Continuous nearly level worn stone-and-earth path extends across the entire width at y=78%-88%, viewed side-on with mild depth. Absolutely no staircase receding into the center and no road vanishing away from camera. Lower middle stays visually calm for a hero and enemies. At least three clearly readable depth planes: sparse dark foreground rock/grass silhouettes at the bottom edge; middle massive trunks, simplified ruined pillars, broken ivy-covered arches; pale cool blue-green trunks and ruin silhouettes receding through atmosphere behind them. Put architectural variation across the frame, leave breathing room in the central walking band. No giant trunk blocking the playable path. Both image edges have comparable ground level, foliage density and tone, but no mirrored composition or obvious repeating giant arch. Environment feels extensive beyond the frame.
Drawing: bold confident angular brush planes, selective hard edges with few soft transitions; TWO TO FOUR large values per object. Moss and foliage in clustered crisp graphic shapes, not individual photoreal leaves. Carved rock expressed by flat facets and a few purposeful linear accents. Deep near-black green/indigo shadows, teal distance, luminous chartreuse canopy, SMALL coral/magenta leaf accents grounded in reference 1. High art-direction quality. No tiny realistic grass, pebbles or moss noise. Avoid pastel softness, paper grain, watercolor wash, thick black outlines, smooth 3D, plastic rendering, photographic texture, photorealism, bloom haze covering everything.
Lighting: clear daytime atmosphere with striking dark-to-light depth and painted broad light shapes; avoid a huge white flare or strong shafts crossing the walking band. This is a composited visual target: any atmosphere or accent lighting here will later be separated into controllable Unity layers, so don't present it as technical layers. No rain, snow, visible sun/moon, glowing magical rings, particles, glowing lanterns, characters, text, watermark, border. The scene must read beautifully when shown as a shallow panoramic strip.
```
